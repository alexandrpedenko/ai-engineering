# Payment policy

A booking is confirmed with a deposit of 20% of the total stay cost, charged
at the time of booking. The remaining 80% is due at the hotel on check-in,
payable by card or cash in the local currency.

Prices shown during search are always per night, before any taxes. A local
tourist tax of €2 per person, per night is added at check-in and is not
included in the total quoted during booking.

We accept Visa, Mastercard, and American Express for the deposit. Some
smaller properties in Porto and Seville do not accept American Express at
the property itself for the remaining balance — check the individual
hotel's page if paying the balance by card matters to the guest.

Deposits are non-transferable between bookings, even for the same guest and
the same property. A cancelled deposit follows the cancellation policy, not
a separate payment policy refund path.
