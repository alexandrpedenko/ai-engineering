# Check-in policy

Standard check-in is from 15:00, and check-out is by 11:00. Arriving earlier
does not guarantee a room is ready; bags can be stored at the front desk free
of charge from any time in the morning.

Late check-in (after 22:00) must be arranged with the property at least a
day in advance — front desks are not staffed 24 hours everywhere, and an
unannounced arrival after that hour may find no one to hand over keys.

A late check-out, up to 13:00, can usually be arranged for free on the day
if the guest asks at breakfast; anything later than 13:00 is billed at 50%
of one night's rate, charged the same way as the remaining balance.

Every guest must present a government-issued photo ID at check-in that
matches the name on the booking. A booking made under one guest's name
cannot be checked in by someone else travelling alone, even if they arrive
with the booking confirmation.
