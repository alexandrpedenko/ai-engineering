# Cancellation policy

Guests can cancel free of charge up to 5 days before check-in. Cancelling
inside that window keeps a refund on the table, but only a partial one: we
charge a fee equal to one night's rate, and refund the rest of what was paid.

Cancelling on the day of check-in, or simply not showing up, forfeits the
full amount — no refund is issued in either case.

Changes to dates count as a cancellation and a new booking if the new dates
fall outside the original rate's validity window. If they fall inside it, we
treat it as a free date change instead, with no fee.

Refunds are returned to the original payment method and typically appear
within 7 business days of the cancellation being processed. We cannot
expedite this — it depends on the guest's bank or card issuer.
