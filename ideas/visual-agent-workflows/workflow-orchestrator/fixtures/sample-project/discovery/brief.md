# Brief: room booking for a co-working space

A co-working space with 12 meeting rooms wants members to book rooms from
their phones instead of the paper sheet at reception.

- Members book a room for a time slot (30-minute steps, 8:00–20:00).
- A member can see which rooms are free right now and later today.
- A booking can be cancelled up to 15 minutes before it starts.
- Reception can see all bookings for the day and free a room that nobody
  turned up for after 10 minutes.
- Members sign in with the email they registered with at the space.

Not decided yet: whether members pay per booking, and whether non-members
(guests) can book. Budget: one developer for six weeks.
