# Customer support policy

Each `##` section below is one retrievable unit. `search_policy` matches a query
against section titles and bodies and returns whole sections — never fragments,
because half a policy clause is worse than none.

## Return window

Items may be returned within 30 days of the order date. The window is measured
from the order date, not the delivery date. Orders outside the window are not
returnable by an assistant under any circumstance; a supervisor may grant an
exception, which means the case goes to a human.

## Returnable order statuses

Only orders that reached the customer can be returned. An order with status
`cancelled` or `processing` was never delivered, so there is nothing to send
back. Status `shipped` and `delivered` are returnable, subject to the return
window.

## Return and refund sequence

An approved return receives an RMA number first. The refund follows, for the
full order total, and is never issued before an RMA exists for the same order.
A refund without an RMA means nobody asked the customer to send the item back,
and the goods are written off silently.

## Refund amounts

A refund must equal the order total exactly. Partial refunds, goodwill credits
and price adjustments are outside an assistant's authority and require a human.
If an order already carries a partial refund, the remaining balance is not an
assistant decision either — hand it to a human.

## Billing disputes

A customer charged for goods they did not receive, charged twice, or charged
after a cancellation has a billing dispute, not a return. Billing disputes are
always handled by a human. Do not authorize a return or issue a refund to
resolve one, and do not promise a specific outcome or timeframe.

## Chargebacks

Once a customer's bank opens a chargeback, the funds are held by the payment
processor and the shop cannot move them. No refund may be issued while a
chargeback is open, because it would pay the same money twice. These cases go
to a human immediately.

## Lost and delayed shipments

A parcel with no carrier scan for 10 days or more is treated as delayed. A
parcel marked `exception` or `returned_to_sender` never reached the customer.
Neither is a return — the customer has nothing to send back — and both are
resolved by a replacement or a refund authorized by a human.

## Delivered to a neighbour or alternate location

A delivery scan recorded at a location other than the customer's address is
still a delivery. If the customer says they never received it, treat it as a
claim a human must assess, not as a return.

## What an assistant may never do

Invent policy, promise an outcome a tool has not yet confirmed, grant an
exception to the return window, issue a partial refund, or move money on a
disputed or charged-back order.
